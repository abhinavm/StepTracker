# Privacy Policy — StepTracker

**Last updated: June 3, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how StepTracker ("the app", "we", "our") handles your information. StepTracker is a personal, independent app with no company or commercial entity behind it.

---

## 1. No Data Collection

StepTracker does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data. The app has no backend server, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no user accounts.

---

## 2. Health Data (Apple HealthKit)

StepTracker reads the following data types from Apple HealthKit:

- **Step count** — to display your daily and weekly step totals
- **Walking + running distance** — to display your distance walked in miles

This data is used solely to display your activity within the app and its home screen and lock screen widgets.

**HealthKit data is:**
- Processed entirely on your device
- Never transmitted to any server
- Never shared with any third party
- Never used for advertising, marketing, or any purpose other than displaying your activity to you

This complies with Apple's HealthKit guidelines, which prohibit using health data for advertising or sharing it with data brokers.

### Revoking HealthKit Access

You can revoke the app's access to your health data at any time:

> **Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → StepTracker**

Revoking access will stop the app from reading new step data. No data is retained by the app after access is revoked — there is nothing to delete because nothing was stored outside your device.

---

## 3. Local Storage

The app stores the following data locally on your device using iOS App Groups (a sandboxed on-device storage mechanism shared between the app and its widgets):

- Your current step count and distance (cached from HealthKit)
- Your configured daily step goal
- Your chosen colour theme (a single hue value, 0.0–1.0)

This data never leaves your device.

---

## 4. Widgets

StepTracker's home screen and lock screen widgets read cached step data from the same on-device App Group storage described above. Widgets do not make any network requests.

---

## 5. No Third-Party SDKs or Services

StepTracker does not include any third-party SDKs, advertising networks, analytics frameworks, or tracking libraries. The only external framework used is Apple's own HealthKit and WidgetKit, governed by Apple's own privacy policies.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

StepTracker does not collect any data from any user, including children under 13. The app is compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) by virtue of collecting no data whatsoever.

---

## 7. Medical Disclaimer

StepTracker is a general wellness and fitness tool for informational purposes only. It is **not a medical device** and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition. Step counts and distance figures are estimates derived from Apple Health and may not be fully accurate. Consult a qualified healthcare professional before beginning any new exercise program.

---

## 8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be updated. Since the app collects no data, no changes could adversely affect your privacy.
